# Water Wise Scientists

A language-responsive Grade 6 Natural Sciences and Technology curriculum (CAPS, Term 2: Matter and Materials).

**Live site:** https://muazahdawson22-dev.github.io/water-wise-scientists/

Three connected units:

1. **What's in the Mix?** Particles, mixtures and separating (weeks 1–2)
2. **Where Did the Sugar Go?** Solutions, dissolving and fair testing (weeks 3–6)
3. **Clean Water for Our Community** Pollution, wetlands and designing a water filter (weeks 6–10)

Each unit has learning outcomes, key words, content, activities with three challenge levels, resources and an assessment with its marking guide. Learners can rate their own progress, build a home-language word bank and listen to the notes read aloud.

Designed by Muazah Ismail for CDD710/733 Curriculum Design and Development (Assignment 3), University of Pretoria.

Reading material: Siyavula *Thunderbolt Kids* Grade 6 Natural Sciences and Technology (free, openly licensed).
